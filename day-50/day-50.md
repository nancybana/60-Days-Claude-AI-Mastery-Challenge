# Day 50: Defend Your Experience with AI

## Overview

Today, I built and tested an AI-powered interview preparation tool using Claude AI. The goal was to simulate real interview scenarios where candidates must confidently explain and defend their skills, projects, achievements, and experiences.

The application generates interview questions based on a user's resume or portfolio and provides a detailed Defense Report highlighting strengths, weak points, and areas for improvement.

## Objective

* Practice defending project work and technical experience
* Improve interview communication and confidence
* Receive AI-generated feedback on responses
* Identify gaps in explanations and project understanding

## Process Followed

1. Opened Claude AI and started a new conversation.
2. Set the effort level to Low.
3. Used the Defend Your Experience prompt.
4. Answered AI-generated interview questions.
5. Generated a complete HTML-based application.
6. Saved and tested the application locally.
7. Uploaded resume/portfolio information.
8. Practiced interview responses.
9. Reviewed the AI-generated Defense Report.
10. Documented key learnings and observations.

## Features of the Application

* Resume and portfolio analysis
* AI-generated interview questions
* Experience validation and defense practice
* Strength and weakness identification
* Defense Report generation
* Interactive HTML interface
* Local browser execution

## Key Learnings

* Strong project understanding is essential during interviews.
* Recruiters often ask follow-up questions to verify experience.
* Clear explanations create more confidence than memorized answers.
* AI can help identify weak areas before actual interviews.
* Communication and technical knowledge must work together.

Successfully created and tested an AI-powered interview defense application. The exercise improved my ability to explain projects, justify technical decisions, and respond confidently to interview questions.
