# Day 36 – Cognitive Pattern Explorer

## Objective

The objective of this activity was to explore how thinking patterns change under different emotional states and situations. The application helped analyze decision-making behavior in both **Calm Mode** and **Stress Mode** through interactive scenarios, drag-and-drop exercises, and reflection activities.

---

## Tasks Completed

* Generated the Cognitive Pattern Explorer HTML application using Claude.
* Saved and opened the application locally in a browser.
* Explored both Calm Mode and Stress Mode experiences.
* Completed Chapter 1 scenario-based responses.
* Finished Chapter 2 drag-and-drop activities.
* Arranged cognitive sequences in Chapter 3.
* Reviewed the personalized Reflection Journal.
* Compared thinking tendencies across all chapters.
* Replayed the experience in the opposite mode.
* Captured screenshots of the Reflection Journal and thinking profile.

---

## Key Learnings

### 1. Thinking Patterns Change With Context

I observed that my responses differed depending on whether I was in Calm Mode or Stress Mode. Emotional state can significantly influence decision-making and judgment.

### 2. Self-Awareness Improves Decision Making

The Reflection Journal highlighted recurring thought patterns and helped identify areas where more deliberate thinking could improve outcomes.

### 3. Structured Reflection Is Valuable

Reviewing decisions after completing activities provided useful insights into personal reasoning habits and cognitive biases.

### 4. Different Perspectives Lead to Different Outcomes

Experiencing the same scenarios in both modes demonstrated how perspective and emotional conditions can alter conclusions.

### 5. Interactive Learning Enhances Understanding

The combination of scenarios, sorting activities, and reflection exercises made abstract cognitive concepts easier to understand and remember.
