## Key Learnings

### Understanding Prior Authorization

* Prior Authorization is a process used by insurance providers to review medical treatments before approval.
* Multiple stakeholders are involved, including patients, healthcare providers, and insurance companies.
* Proper documentation plays a critical role in obtaining approval.
* Communication delays and missing information can slow the process.

### Product & UX Insights

* Story-based learning makes complex healthcare workflows easier to understand.
* Interactive choices increase user engagement.
* Progress indicators help users track completion status.
* Visual storytelling improves retention of information.
