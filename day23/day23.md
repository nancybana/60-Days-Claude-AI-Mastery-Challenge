# Day 23 - Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Startup Idea
AI-Powered Loan Transparency and Government Scheme Assistance Platform

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Loan applicants
- Students seeking education loans
- Small business owners
- Government scheme beneficiaries

## Buyer Persona
Name: Ravi Kumar
Age: 28
Occupation: Small Business Owner
Goal: Understand loan costs and government scheme eligibility.

## Top Customer Pain Points
1. Hidden charges
2. Unclear interest calculations
3. EMI confusion
4. Complex documents
5. Eligibility uncertainty
6. Missed deadlines
7. Penalty confusion
8. Lack of transparency
9. Multiple information sources
10. Poor financial awareness

## Customer Journey
Awareness → Research → Application → Approval → Repayment

## Buying Triggers
- Need for a loan
- Need for government assistance
- Financial planning

## Customer Objections
- Data privacy concerns
- Accuracy concerns
- Trust issues

## MVP Features
- Loan calculator
- EMI tracker
- Scheme eligibility checker
- Document checklist

## MoSCoW Prioritization

### Must Have
- Loan calculator
- Eligibility checker

### Should Have
- EMI reminders

### Could Have
- AI chatbot

### Won't Have
- Advanced analytics

## Pricing Hypothesis
- Free basic plan
- Premium plan for advanced insights

## Top Risks
1. Incorrect recommendations
2. Regulatory changes
3. User trust
4. Data security
5. Low adoption

## Founder Action Sheet
- Interview 10 potential users
- Build MVP prototype
- Collect feedback
- Improve features

## Key Learnings
- Customer understanding is essential.
- MVP should focus on core problems.
- Validation reduces startup risk.
