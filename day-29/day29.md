# Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Simulation

## Overview

Today’s challenge focused on managing a real-world supply chain crisis through an interactive business simulation. The objective was to analyze disruptions, make strategic decisions under pressure, negotiate with suppliers, answer leadership questions, and invest in AI-driven solutions to improve operational resilience.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze a randomly generated company profile.
* Identify and understand the assigned supply chain crisis.
* Evaluate multiple response strategies.
* Select the most effective crisis management actions.
* Complete supplier negotiation rounds.
* Demonstrate leadership decision-making.
* Invest in AI technologies to strengthen supply chain operations.
* Review business performance through an executive dashboard.

---

## Simulation Workflow

1. Opened the simulation environment.
2. Reviewed the generated company profile.
3. Analyzed the assigned supply chain disruption.
4. Selected three crisis response actions.
5. Negotiated with suppliers across multiple rounds.
6. Responded to CEO leadership questions.
7. Chose two AI-powered supply chain investments.
8. Evaluated final business outcomes and performance metrics.
9. Replayed the simulation with different strategies to compare results.

---

## Key Concepts Learned

### Supply Chain Risk Management

Understanding how disruptions impact inventory, production, customer satisfaction, and profitability.

### Strategic Decision-Making

Making business decisions under uncertainty while balancing short-term recovery and long-term growth.

### Supplier Negotiation

Learning how communication and negotiation influence operational continuity and costs.

### Leadership During Crisis

Evaluating trade-offs and making decisions that align with organizational goals.

### AI in Supply Chain Management

Exploring how artificial intelligence can improve forecasting, risk detection, logistics optimization, and operational efficiency.
