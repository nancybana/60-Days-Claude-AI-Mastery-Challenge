# Learning Notes

## What is Chain-of-Thought Prompting?

Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting is a technique where AI is guided to reason through a problem step-by-step before producing a final answer.

## Benefits

- Better reasoning
- More detailed outputs
- Improved planning
- Better personalization
- Reduced chances of missing important factors

## My Experiment

I generated a career roadmap using:

1. A normal prompt
2. A Chain-of-Thought prompt

## Observations

### Normal Prompt
- Direct answer
- General roadmap
- Limited personalization

### Chain-of-Thought Prompt
- Analyzed current position
- Identified strengths and weaknesses
- Found skill gaps
- Created a detailed roadmap
- Added internship milestones

## Conclusion

The Chain-of-Thought prompt produced a more structured, personalized, and actionable career roadmap compared to the normal prompt.
