# Day 30 – Supply Chain Optimizer

## Objective

Build and explore a Supply Chain Optimizer simulation using Claude-generated React HTML application. The goal was to understand how supply chain decisions impact business performance, costs, efficiency, risk, and overall optimization.

---

## Key Learnings

### 1. Supply Chain Decisions Are Interconnected

A single decision can affect multiple business metrics including cost, efficiency, delivery performance, and risk.

### 2. Cost Optimization Is Not Always Best

The cheapest option may introduce higher operational risks or lower customer satisfaction.

### 3. Inventory Management Matters

Maintaining the right inventory balance helps reduce storage costs while avoiding stock shortages.

### 4. Transportation Choices Impact Performance

Faster transportation improves service levels but can increase operational expenses.

### 5. Data-Driven Decision Making

Successful supply chains rely on balancing multiple factors rather than optimizing only one metric.

### 6. Trade-Off Analysis Is Critical

Every strategic choice has benefits and drawbacks that must be evaluated carefully.
